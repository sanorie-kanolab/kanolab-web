People

Connecting research and practice across disciplines and institutions.

Principal Investigator｜Kei Kano

Professor, Science Communication Laboratory, Faculty of Education, Shiga University Visiting Fellow, Australian National Centre for the Public Awareness of Science, Australian National University

Kei Kano works at the intersection of science, education, society and policy, with a particular focus on making science communication and STEM/STEAM education more inclusive, culturally relevant and responsive to social change.

Kano holds a PhD in life science and has worked as a life-science researcher, part-time high-school teacher, preparatory-school instructor and university professor. Across disciplinary and institutional boundaries, Kano collaborates with researchers, educators, broadcasters, policymakers and community organisations to connect research with educational practice, public engagement, resources, media and policy.

Three research areas

1｜Inclusive Science Communication and STEAM Education

  • Inclusion of people with low interest in science and technology
  • Women in STEM
  • Inclusion of deaf and hard-of-hearing children and their families
  • Culturally relevant STEAM education

2｜Traditional and Indigenous Knowledge for a Sustainable Society

Kano’s current work centres on Kakishibu and tea and connects traditional knowledge, local culture, sustainability education and green innovation. Earlier work has also used Kyoto vegetables and wagashi in culturally grounded science learning.

3｜Responsible AI in Education

Kano studies the design, introduction and governance of AI and EdTech through teacher and learner agency, educational data, ELSI/RRI, inclusion, equity and accountability.

UNESCO Chair

As Chairholder of the UNESCO Chair on STEAM Education for Braiding Traditional Cultural Resources with Science, Kano leads international research, education, community engagement and mobility connecting education, culture, and science, technology and innovation.

International and professional roles

  • Member, UNESCO International Expert Group on Scientific Literacy and Science Popularisation
  • Member, PCST Network Scientific Committee, 2025–2029
  • Co-chair, PCST Network Diversity, Equity, Inclusion and Accessibility Committee
  • Contributor to the OECD Teaching Compass
  • Visiting Fellow, Australian National Centre for the Public Awareness of Science, Australian National University

Educational television and media collaboration

Kei Kano served on the programme committees of NHK Educational TV’s Think Like a Crow—The Scientific Method and Viewpoint Science, contributing to production, development and research support. The programmes’ approaches were extended into lessons, workshops, a card game and books.

Selected awards

Kano received the Prize for Science and Technology from Japan’s Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology twice, in 2014 and 2017, for achievements in science communication practice and research. Other recognition includes awards from the Japan Society for Science Education for practice, conference presentations and audience-diversity research.
