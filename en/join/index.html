Join the Lab

Bring your question into an international and interdisciplinary research environment.

The Kano Lab welcomes prospective PhD students, early-career researchers considering a JSPS fellowship, visiting researchers and collaborators from around the world.

We are not looking for people who will simply repeat the lab’s existing themes. We value the expertise, experience, place, language and questions that each person brings—and the new research connections that can be created with the lab’s work.

Three routes to participation

PhD study

We consider possible supervision in accordance with the formal doctoral programme, admissions requirements and application procedures in which Shiga University participates. Before a formal application, we carefully discuss the research topic, methods, degree route, language, funding and timing.

Information for prospective PhD students

JSPS fellowships

When a topic and hosting environment are a strong match, we may consider developing a joint application to a JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowship for Research in Japan. The candidate and Japanese host researcher co-develop the research plan, followed by the host institution’s procedures.

Information for prospective JSPS fellows

Visiting research, collaboration and professional activities

We welcome enquiries about short research visits, international collaboration, projects with schools, communities and companies, invited lectures, media commentary, expert groups and advisory work.

Explore collaboration and visits

Research themes of particular interest

  • Science communication including people with low interest in science and technology
  • Women in STEM and gender inclusion
  • Inclusive STEM with deaf and hard-of-hearing children and their families
  • Culturally relevant STEAM education
  • Kakishibu, traditional knowledge, Indigenous knowledge and sustainability
  • Science and culture through Kyoto vegetables, tea and wagashi
  • Responsible AI in education, educational data and ELSI/RRI
  • Learning through science television, video, games and books
  • Workshops, science cafés, art–science and audience evaluation
  • Public engagement, deliberative public comment and policy communication
  • Game-based learning, accessibility and multilingual adaptation

Opportunities connected with the UNESCO Chair

PhD, JSPS, visiting and collaborative research proposals may connect with the UNESCO Chair. Possible combinations include Kakishibu, tea, traditional cultural resources, cultural safeguarding, ESD, teacher learning, comparative research, resource design and community co-creation.

Explore the UNESCO Chair

What researchers may expect

What can be provided depends on status, programme, duration and project.

  • Regular discussion of the research plan
  • Lab or related seminars and interdisciplinary feedback
  • Advice on international conferences, English-language papers and proposals
  • Practice-based research with schools, families, communities, companies, media and policy
  • Opportunities to create diverse outputs, including resources, games, workshops and books
  • Attention to ethics, data management, accessibility and collaboration agreements

Values that guide the lab

  • Respect for different expertise and lived experience
  • Equitable relationships with participants, communities and knowledge holders
  • Accessibility and multiple ways of communicating
  • Returning research benefits and findings
  • A culture that can acknowledge uncertainty, limitations and the need to learn again

Before contacting us

PhD and JSPS candidates should prepare the materials listed on the respective detailed pages. For a general collaboration, please provide a one- or two-page outline of the purpose, topic, expected role of the Kano Lab, timing, funding and intended outcomes.
