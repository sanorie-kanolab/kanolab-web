Social Media and Media

Sharing both the process and outcomes of research in open and accessible forms.

The Kano Lab uses each medium for a different purpose. This website is the reference point for important research, application and event information, and social-media posts should link back to the relevant page.

Instagram｜Images and short videos from our activities

Instagram shares workshops, behind-the-scenes development, resources, lab activity and event announcements. Add captions and alternative text so that meaning does not depend on the image alone.

https://www.instagram.com/kanolab_shigauniv/

note｜Longer reports and reflections

note carries the background to projects, the process of developing workshops, event reports and the intentions behind resources—material that needs more space than a short social-media post.

https://note.com/kanolab

Media and speaking enquiries

For interviews, television, articles, lectures, panels and expert commentary, use the Contact page. Include the theme, outlet, audience, publication date, role required and deadline.

Media and speaking enquiries

Principles for posts and images

  • Use photographs of children and participants only within the scope of consent.
  • Provide captions or content descriptions for video wherever possible.
  • Distinguish preliminary findings from established conclusions.
  • Credit partners, production teams, communities and collaborators.
  • Link posts to stable official information even after the post becomes old.
